        Initial message templates

        These are the 6 categories the dashboard suggests based on a profile's stats.         Timing is days since the phase started. {naam} becomes the lead's first name, {link} your calendar link. Each step's wording is composed from several phrasings, so leads due for the same step at the same time don't all get the identical nudge.
